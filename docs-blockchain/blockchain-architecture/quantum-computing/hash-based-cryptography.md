# Hash‑Based Cryptography

## Hash‑Based Cryptography

<mark style="color:violet;background-color:pink;">**Principle: security from cryptographic hashes**</mark>

* Used for digital signatures and other cryptographic primitives
* believed quantum‑resistant — due to the unique structure of their security proofs
  * <mark style="background-color:red;">**could not be too**</mark>



<mark style="background-color:blue;">**Example**</mark>: `XMSS`: `Extended Merkle Signature Scheme`
