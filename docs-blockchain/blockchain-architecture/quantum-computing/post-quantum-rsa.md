# Post‑Quantum RSA

## Post‑Quantum RSA&#x20;

> **Post‑Quantum RSA:**
>
> variants of **`RSA`**: `Rivest-Shamir-Adleman` encryption

<mark style="background-color:blue;">**Approach**</mark>: <mark style="color:blue;">**larger key sizes**</mark> for increased resistance against quantum attacks

<mark style="background-color:blue;">**Tradeoff**</mark>: <mark style="background-color:red;">**lower efficiency**</mark> vs other quantum-resistant  alternatives
