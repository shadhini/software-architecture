# QKD: Quantum Key Distribution

## `QKD`: Quantum Key Distribution&#x20;

Not a classical encryption algorithm — <mark style="color:violet;">**quantum protocol**</mark>



<mark style="background-color:blue;">**Examples**</mark>: `BB84`, `QDSA`

<mark style="background-color:blue;">**Purpose**</mark>: <mark style="color:blue;">**quantum‑secure key distribution**</mark>

* use quantum properties to distribute cryptographic keys securely

<mark style="background-color:blue;">**Property**</mark>: <mark style="color:green;">**resistant to eavesdropping**</mark> even by quantum computers
