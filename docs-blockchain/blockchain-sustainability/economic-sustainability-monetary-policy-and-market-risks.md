# Economic Sustainability, Monetary Policy & Market Risks

## Economic Sustainability, Monetary Policy & Market Risks

economics of native tokens, stablecoins, CBDCs, commercial banking impacts, and end-user market dynamics



### Robustness of Blockchain as a form of Currency&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Weigh pros vs cons** before using cryptocurrencies as investment or everyday currency.
{% endhint %}

<mark style="background-color:green;">adoption & utility</mark>: widespread adoption, practical utility of technology → economic sustainability, increase market efficiency, create opportunities for long-term growth & resilience

<mark style="background-color:red;">long term viability concerns for crypto assets:</mark> [market saturation](#user-content-fn-1)[^1], regulatory measures, adoption rates

#### Concerns that Hinder Adoption

* Environmental impact: high energy consumption → negative environmental perception
* Privacy & anonymity: public ledger transparency → deanonymization risk, partial anonymity and transaction traceability
* Security & custody: network & ecosystem attacks
* Technical contraints: steep learning curves, technological barriers: device/internet access, digital divide, wallet management complexity
* Regulatory & Legal: overly restrictive/uncertain regulations → hinder adoption & sustainability while clear & supportive regulations → stability



### Monetary Authority & Macroeconomic Impact

* Central Bank Disruption: <kbd>Digital assets</kbd>, <kbd>cryptocurrencies</kbd> and <kbd>stablecoins</kbd> <mark style="background-color:red;">**challenge central bank authority, monetary policy, state economic management**</mark>.
* Central Bank Digital Currencies (<kbd>**CBDC**</kbd>**s**): Policy shift toward state-issued digital currencies, raise concerns regarding  <mark style="background-color:red;">**government financial surveillance**</mark>, <mark style="background-color:red;">**consumer privacy**</mark> and control.
* <mark style="background-color:red;">**Financial**</mark> <mark style="background-color:red;">**Intermediary**</mark> Disruption: <mark style="background-color:red;">**Revenue loss**</mark> for **traditional banks and payment processors** from peer-to-peer settlement networks.



### Market Dynamics & Volatility

* <mark style="background-color:red;">Store of Value Limitations</mark>: Price swings, market manipulation by larger holders (whales), and low liquidity[^2] impeding everyday **unit-of-account utility**.
  * → **discourage** everyday payment **adoption** (use as a stable store of value or medium of exchange)&#x20;
  * → position crypto primarily as <mark style="background-color:red;">**speculative assets**</mark>(instability)
  * → perception of cryptocurrencies as investment assets rather than currency
* <mark style="background-color:red;">Settlement & Fee Friction</mark>:&#x20;
  * scalability bottlenecks
  * transaction latency: low throughput (TPS limits), slow confirmation time under load
  * fee spikes during network congestion



### Consumer Protection & Network Usability

* <mark style="background-color:red;">Irreversibility & Custody Risks</mark>:&#x20;
  * Irreversible transactions mean <mark style="background-color:red;">**lost keys or misdirected funds cannot be recovered.**</mark>
  * The ecosystem <mark style="background-color:red;">**lacks standard consumer safety nets**</mark> such as deposit insurance, chargebacks, or central customer support.
* <mark style="background-color:red;">Cross-Chain Fragmentation</mark>: Network <mark style="background-color:red;">**hard forks**</mark> and a <mark style="background-color:red;">**lack of cross-chain interoperability**</mark> fragment asset flow and reduce long-term utility.



[^1]: a business state where the volume of a product or service available in the marketplace meets or exceeds customer demand

[^2]: availability of liquid assets; cash or assets that can be quickly, securely, and easily converted into cash without a significant loss in market value
