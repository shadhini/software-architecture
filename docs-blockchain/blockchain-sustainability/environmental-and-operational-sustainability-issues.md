# Environmental & Operational Sustainability Issues

## Environmental & Operational Sustainability Issues



### Carbon Footprint & Energy Dynamics

#### Consensus Mechanism Efficiency

* Proof of Stake (<mark style="background-color:green;">**PoS**</mark>) consumes <mark style="background-color:green;">**significantly less energy**</mark> than proof of Work (**PoW**).
  * PoS networks — validators consume electricity
* **PoW** consensus mechanisms **intentionally trade efficiency for security and decentralization**, as centralized systems avoid redundant data, consensus activities, heavy data sync and strong cryptographic signatures.



#### Energy Consumption & Metrics&#x20;

<mark style="background-color:red;">annual energy consumption</mark>

* <kbd>Bitcoin</kbd>: \~ 150.97 TWh&#x20;
  * exceeds major tech giants combined (Apple, Amazon, Facebook, Microsoft, Google)
  * ≈ total consumption of all televisions and refrigerators in the US
* <kbd>Ethereum</kbd>: \~ 5.89 GWh (\~0.004% of Bitcoin)



<mark style="background-color:red;">ledger sizes</mark>

* Bitcoin ledger: 475 GB
  * 300,000 miners storing this data → 475GB x 300,000 miners = 142,500 TB of data
* Ethereum ledger: 1,156 GB
* ❓Question: ledger data worth the cost long‑term?
* ✅ Exploration: <mark style="background-color:green;">**off‑load historical data**</mark> + <mark style="background-color:green;">**keep cryptographic proofs on the ledger**</mark>



<mark style="background-color:red;">NFT minting (a single NFT): carbon ≈ 500-mile road trip</mark>



#### Energy Sources & Geography&#x20;

* Fossil fuels power \~60% of Bitcoin mining.
* Mining operations in cheap electricity hubs relying on **fossil fuels** generate a <mark style="background-color:red;">**high carbon footprint**</mark>, whereas locations using <mark style="color:$success;">**renewables**</mark> (<mark style="background-color:green;">renewable energy sources</mark>)(e.g: <mark style="background-color:green;">**hydroelectric**</mark>) mitigate emissions.



#### Node & Smart Contract Compute&#x20;

* **Complex** (complex calculations, frequent updates) or <mark style="background-color:red;">**un-optimized smart contracts**</mark> execute individually across most network nodes, creating **unnecessary compute overhead**.
* <mark style="background-color:red;">**Resource-intensive dApps**</mark> (gaming, large data) and <mark style="background-color:red;">**node cooling**</mark> in data centers add further energy demands.



### Hardware Lifecycle & e-Waste Management

eWaste sources:

* large-scale (cryptocurrency) mining farms — <mark style="background-color:red;">**major source**</mark>
* dApps (gaming, large data) — resource-intensive hardware needs
* validator nodes (PoS too)
* blockchain devices / hardware wallets
* blockchain integration in IoT/ AI/ hardware devices — <mark style="background-color:$warning;">**compounded e-waste**</mark>

#### Hardware Turnover & Obsolescence&#x20;

* **PoW mining hardware** is <mark style="background-color:red;">**replaced roughly every 14 months**</mark> due to rapid tech advancement (shorter lifespan for cutting-edge technologies → \~3-5 years of consumer device cycle) leading to frequent hardware obsolescence, leapfrogging, and specialized multi-algorithm.
  * <mark style="background-color:red;">**rapid hardware turnover**</mark>
  * ‼️ outdated mining hardware becomes obsolete before proper disposal practices are established
  * ‼️ inadequate planning for the lifecycle of mining hardware
* Annual hardware e-waste matches the trash output of \~600,000 people.



#### Production & Environmental Risks

* **Raw material extraction** (silicon, copper, rare metals) for hardware component production contributes to <mark style="background-color:red;">**resource depletion**</mark> and <mark style="background-color:red;">**ethical supply-chain risks**</mark> via conflict minerals (associated with exploitation, human rights abuses).
* **hardware manufacturing** <mark style="background-color:red;">**emissions**</mark>, hardware/raw **material** **transport** emissions
* Unregulated dumping or improper disposal <mark style="background-color:red;">**leaches heavy metals into soil and water**</mark>.
  * → environmental pollution, health hazards



#### Global Distribution & Logistics&#x20;

* Export to countries with lax[^1] regs
* Unregulated e‑waste import
* Inequitable impact on communities near mining operations --> health risks & environmental injustice



#### Disposal, Recycling & Technical Challenges

* **Recycling** **costs** often <mark style="background-color:red;">**exceed recovered material value**</mark>.
  * lack of incentives for reuse/refurbishment /e-waste management
* <mark style="background-color:red;">**Proper recycling**</mark> is <mark style="background-color:red;">**hindered**</mark> by&#x20;
  * complex hardware disassembly
  * hardware fragmentation — diverse components
  * missing extended producer responsibility standards
    * that hold manufacturers accountable for the disposal and recycling of their products
  * lack of industry e-waste management standards / practices / metrics
    * difficult to estimate the future volume of e-waste  → challenges for waste management planning
  * lack of recycling / e-waste management infrastructure
  * data privacy risks on discarded storage devices&#x20;
  * energy-intensive recycling processes



### Externanlities, Centralization & Standards

* Energy usage creates <mark style="color:red;background-color:red;">**negative externalities**</mark> where <mark style="background-color:red;">**environmental costs**</mark> are <mark style="background-color:red;">**shared**</mark> <mark style="background-color:red;">**globally**</mark> while **financial benefits** [**accrue**](#user-content-fn-2)[^2] **to crypto holders**.
* <mark style="color:red;background-color:red;">**Perceived exclusivity**</mark>: Access & participation favors those with cheap energy → <mark style="background-color:red;">**centralization**</mark> (cheap electricity hubs, mining farms, economies of scale).
* Decentralized networks currently <mark style="background-color:red;">**lack universal standards**</mark> for **measuring and reporting environmental impact**.
  * Defining universal standards is hard on decentralized, open‑source tech.

scaling (users, transactions), node data expansion e.g., multi-terabyte requirements)  —  <mark style="background-color:red;">**network growth**</mark>&#x20;
